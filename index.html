Where Do You Stand?

Eighteen statements across three independent spectrums — not a single left/right label, but a graduated position on each.

This is a quick, simplified reflection tool, not a scientific instrument. Instead of sorting you into "left" or "right," it places you at a continuous point along three separate spectrums: how you view the economy, how you weigh personal liberty against social order, and how you feel about tradition versus change.

Answer honestly and quickly — first instinct is usually best. Most real people land somewhere in the middle stretch of each spectrum, not at either end.
